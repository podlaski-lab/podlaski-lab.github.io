We are always looking for driven, mathematically inclined researchers eager to tackle questions at the interface of neural engineering, neuroimaging, and theoretical neuroscience.

### 1. Graduate Students (M.S., Ph.D., Integrated)
Prospective graduate students should apply via the **Department of Brain Science and Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU)**.
* **Qualifications:** Quantitative background (Engineering, CS, Physics, Applied Math, Neuroscience, or related fields) with programming proficiency (Python, MATLAB, or C/C++).
* **How to Apply:** Send an email to `your-email@skku.edu` with your CV, academic transcripts, and a concise statement (1 page) explaining your research interests and why you want to join the NET Lab.

### 2. Postdoctoral Researchers
Postdoctoral fellows can be supported through research grants via SKKU and CNIR / IBS.
* Candidates should possess a Ph.D. in a relevant discipline and a track record of publications.
* Please submit a CV, a brief research statement detailing your technical accomplishments and future research plan, and contact information for 2–3 references.

### 3. Undergraduate Interns
We welcome motivated undergraduate students from SKKU and other institutions for semester or summer/winter research internships.
* Prior experience with linear algebra, signal processing, or Python/PyTorch is preferred.

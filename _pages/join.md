We are looking for motivated, mathematically-inclined researchers interested in questions in systems & computational neuroscience and NeuroAI.

### Graduate Students (M.S., Ph.D., Integrated)
Prospective graduate students should apply to join the NET lab through the **Department of Brain Science and Engineering (BSE) at Sungkyunkwan University**. Because there are no lab rotations, applicants must apply directly to a chosen lab with the PI's prior support.
* **Qualifications:** A quantitative background (computational neuroscience, physics, mathematics, engineering, computer science, or a related field), or a neuroscience/biology background paired with a strong motivation to learn the necessary math. Programming experience is essential.
* **How to Apply:** Send an email to `wf.podlaski@skku.edu` with your CV and a concise statement (~1 page) explaining your research interests and why you want to join the NET lab. Admissions run twice a year (for spring and fall entry). Please reach out around July–August or February–March if possible, though inquiries are welcome year-round.

### Research assistants and interns
We regularly take on students and independent researchers as RAs and interns for shorter-term projects. Please get in touch by sending your CV along with a brief note about your interests and availability. Preference is given to students already based in Korea, though exceptions can be made.

### Postdoctoral researchers
We do not currently have funding for a dedicated postdoctoral opening, but I am happy to sponsor candidates applying for independent fellowships. Relevant programs include the NRF Sejong Science Fellowship and Postdoctoral Domestic Training (for domestic/Korean applicants), the NRF Brain Pool Program, HFSP, MSCA Global Fellowships, and EMBO Fellowships (for international applicants), as well as the SKKU President Fellowship. If you are interested in joining the lab, please get in touch with your CV for more details at least 6 months before your target start date so we can discuss options and application timelines.

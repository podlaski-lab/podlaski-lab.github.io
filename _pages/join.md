We are looking for motivated, mathematically-inclined researchers interested in questions in systems & computational neuroscience and NeuroAI.

### Graduate Students (MSc, PhD, Integrated)
Prospective graduate students should apply to join the NET lab through the **Department of Brain Science and Engineering (BSE) at Sungkyunkwan University**. Because there are no lab rotations, applicants must apply directly to a chosen lab with the PI's prior support. Admitted MSc, PhD, and integrated students are typically funded, receiving a monthly living stipend alongside tuition support/scholarships.
* **Qualifications:** A quantitative background (computational neuroscience, physics, mathematics, engineering, computer science, or a related field), or a neuroscience/biology background paired with a strong motivation to learn the necessary math. Programming experience is essential.
* **How to Apply:** Send an email to `wf.podlaski@skku.edu` with your CV, a concise statement (~1 page) explaining your research interests and why you want to join the NET lab, and contact information for 1–2 academic references (such as previous research advisors or course instructors, if available). Admissions run twice a year: please reach out around July–August (for Sept-Oct application period and Spring entry) or January-February (for Mar-Apr application period and Fall entry), though inquiries are welcome year-round.
* **Official SKKU Admissions Info:** Application guidelines, exact deadlines, and eligibility criteria
  * International applicants: See the SKKU [International Graduate Admissions portal](https://admission-global.skku.edu/eng/) (note that there are typically two application rounds per semester).
  * Korean / domestic applicants: See the SKKU [General Graduate Admissions portal](https://gradschool.skku.edu/grad/index.htm).
  * Department info: Visit the [Department of Brain Science and Engineering](https://ics.skku.edu/eng_icon/bse_science_intro.do) ([뇌과학·뇌공학과](https://bse.skku.edu/bse/index.do)) for program and curriculum details.

### Research assistants, interns, and visitors
We regularly take on undergraduate students (including SKKU students through URP or semester internships), recent graduates, or other researchers as RAs, interns, or visitors for shorter-term projects—typically lasting at least 2–3 months. Because computational projects require solid coding skills, prior programming experience is expected. Positions are primarily in-person at SKKU, so preference is given to students already based in Korea, though remote exceptions can occasionally be made. To apply, send your CV to `wf.podlaski@skku.edu` along with a brief note about your research interests, your availability (start date, duration, and hours per week), and a GitHub link if you have one.

### Postdoctoral researchers
We do not currently have dedicated funding for an open postdoctoral position, but I am happy to sponsor strong candidates applying for independent fellowships. Relevant programs include:
* Domestic / Korean applicants: NRF Sejong Science Fellowship and Postdoctoral Domestic Training (박사후국내연수) (via [NRF](https://www.nrf.re.kr/) / [IRIS](https://www.iris.go.kr/)), and the SKKU President Fellowship.
* International / overseas applicants: [NRF Brain Pool Program](https://eng.nrf.re.kr/page/5301bb32-ad33-4031-8de1-a8b3275956a2?bizNo=5), [HFSP Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.hfsp.org/funding/hfsp-funding/postdoctoral-fellowships), [MSCA Global Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships), [EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/), and the SKKU President Fellowship.

If you are interested in joining the lab, please reach out to `wf.podlaski@skku.edu` with your CV, brief research ideas, and contact details for 2 references—ideally at least 6 months before your target start date so we can plan around application timelines.

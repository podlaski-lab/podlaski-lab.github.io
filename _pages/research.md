  <div class="col-sm-8">
    <h3>2. Neural Interfaces & Decoding Algorithms</h3>
    <p>
      Designing closed-loop neural decoding systems and real-time algorithms for bidirectional brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), aiming to restore cognitive or motor capabilities.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

  <div class="col-sm-8">
    <h3>3. Multimodal Functional Neuroimaging</h3>
    <p>
      Leveraging CNIR's ultra-high-field (7T/9.4T) animal and human MRI facilities combined with electrophysiology to investigate cross-scale neural dynamics from microcircuits to whole-brain networks.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>
<hr>

    <h3>4. Machine Learning & Neuro-AI</h3>
    <p>
      Utilizing deep representation learning, latent variable models, and self-supervised architectures to extract low-dimensional biological structures from high-dimensional neural and behavioral datasets.
    </p>
